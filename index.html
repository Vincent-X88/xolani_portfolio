Maximize: Nova Sound Pro

My goal was to make the product page as informative as possible without cluttering the screen... For this project, I designed a mobile e-commerce experience for a brand called Maximize, specifically focusing on their 'Nova Sound Pro' headphones. My goal was to make the product page as informative as possible without cluttering the screen. I structured the layout to guide the user naturally from the product imagery down to the color selection and price. I made sure to include a detailed 'Specification' section with clear icons—like 'Water-Proof' and 'Fast Charge'—so users can scan features quickly. To build trust and reduce hesitation, I also integrated a 'Product Support & FAQs' section and a user testimonial right near the bottom, ensuring customers feel confident before hitting that 'Add To Cart' button.

Branding

Hornets: Esports & Team Branding

For the Hornets identity project, I aimed to create a bold, aggressive brand look suitable for a competitive sports or esports team. I designed the logo around a sharp, stylized hornet head enclosed in a hexagon... For the Hornets identity project, I aimed to create a bold, aggressive brand look suitable for a competitive sports or esports team. I designed the logo around a sharp, stylized hornet head enclosed in a hexagon, using a high-contrast palette of black, white, and electric yellow to make it pop. My main focus was versatility; I wanted to ensure the logo worked just as well as a small embroidered patch on a cap as it did as a large chest print on a t-shirt. I applied the branding across a full range of merchandise—including polos and tees—to demonstrate how the identity system holds up across different fabrics and formats.
